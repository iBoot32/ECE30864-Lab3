Pro: Agile is an iterative approach, meaning you break a project down into discrete and manageable stages, and distribute work in smaller teams.
Pro: Agile is more resilint to scope changes in a project than other methods because of the small team dynamic and collaboration with customers.
Con: Iterativeness means it can be hard to predict project timelines and especially budget, since requirements will change dynamically.
Con: Agile relies very much on feedback from customers, which can lead to longer development times, and relies on the customer to be prompt and communicate with the team routinely.

3. 
a) Sprint Planning Meeting: Determine what to complete in the coming sprint, assign stories to the team and move from the backlog to the current sprint
b) Daily Scrum: Short meeting happening every day of the sprint, around 10 minutes just to discuss completed work and discuss any blockers and planned work.
c) Spring retroactive meeting: discuss completed work and move incomplete work to the backlog, and close the sprint out.

4. This is kind of a hierarchy of issue scope. An epic is the largest scope, which is a larger body of work which is broken down into smaller stories to accomplish this overarching goal. The story is a subdivision of the epic which is a system requirement expressed in a few short sentences. A bug is the smallest unit of work, which is just a specific fix or improvement to something as a blocker, not necessarily a larger system requirement. FInally, a task is a smaller piece of work for a developer to work on, typically taken from a story's functional requirement.
